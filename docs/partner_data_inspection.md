The raw files are unchanged. Numeric report blanks mean **unknown**, never confirmed zero. No customer identifiers or historical stockout-duration columns were found.

## Exact joins and field sources

- All tables join on the trimmed **string 1C code**: monthly `Номенклатура.Код` ↔ document `Код` ↔ transit `Код 1с` ↔ IEK MOQ `Код 1с` / Systeme MOQ `Номенклатура.Код`. No fuzzy names/articles. The resulting catalog union is 3,185 IEK and 724 Systeme Electric codes; no cross-brand code collisions were found. Products without usable sales are retained with unknown forecasts.
- The supplier/brand is the explicitly inspected workbook family, `IEK` or `Systeme Electric`. This is a brand grouping, not a verified supplier legal entity. Category comes only from Systeme's `Категория 2026` where present. IEK category stays missing.
- Month headers are parsed from their Russian month prefix and four-digit year. Unpivot to one SKU/month. Monthly sales are primary; an observed signed outbound-document sum may fill a blank. Missing periods/negative net months remain in the normalization audit and do not become zero demand. If any shipment quantity in a month is missing, its observed partial document sum is retained but cannot fill a blank monthly value or establish reconciliation.
- `document_id` is `Номер` plus the document calendar date, with original timestamp, type, unit and warehouse retained. Multiple signed lines aggregate per brand/SKU/document/warehouse before detection; their quantities and line count remain auditable. Optional future anonymized customer IDs can be retained, not inferred from documents.
- IEK current stock is its latest September inventory cell, never a carried-forward older cell. Systeme uses the same fallback and prefers the 22 September `Свободный остаток` for its 497 available snapshot products. Current snapshot zero does **not** prove a historical whole-month stockout.
- IEK inbound quantities come from six columns: РФ УТ-7583 (arrival by 10 October), РФ УТ-7974 (15 October), ПП УТ-7848 (1 October), УТ-8231/8233/8234 (30 September), all 2026. Systeme inbound comes only from `СЭ в пути 24.09`, arrival 24 September 2026. Dates are retained, but the existing engine assumes all reported transit arrives within the chosen horizon; verify timing before approval.
- Systeme stock components `Витрина`, `Остаток ТЗ`, `РЦ ЕКТ  Рыскулова`, `Розничный склад` are preserved separately. They cannot be consistently aligned with all historical warehouses, so recommendations do not pretend to be warehouse-specific.
- IEK MOQ `Мин. разр. к отгр.` → `minimum_order_qty`; Systeme dedicated `Кратность` → `order_multiple`. Conflicting duplicate constraints become unavailable rather than arbitrary; consistent duplicates collapse. No MOQ is fabricated for unmatched codes.
- `СЕЗОННОСТЬ` column L supplies 12 monthly factors, mapped only to the appropriate brand. Baseline-month normalization avoids applying the same seasonal level twice. History-based SKU/category seasonality remains the fallback.

## Fields deliberately not used as calculation inputs

No supplier lead time or confirmed purchase-price column exists. `lead_time_days` and `unit_price` remain missing. A sidebar lead time is explicitly a **manager planning assumption** and results remain REVIEW. `СС реал` is preserved as `reported_cost`, not treated as a supplier unit price. Provided `Заказ`, `Запас`, `Кэф Роста`, `Кэф Сез-ти`, aggregate sales sums and unlabeled auxiliary cells do not replace the auditable engine's calculations. Missing-code rows cannot be safely joined.

The monthly reports and document scopes do not fully reconcile: for nonblank 2025 months, all signed document totals matched 7,909/12,951 IEK and 1,377/4,022 Systeme entries during inspection. Consequently a document spike is subtracted only on an exact monthly reconciliation and only if subtraction would not make net sales negative. Otherwise its flag requires review. Statistical isolation is not proof of commercial intent.

September 2026 is partial and excluded from baseline/variability/growth fitting, while retained in audits. Earlier-than-2024 documents remain auditable but do not extend the 33-month normalized monthly grid. Missing sales cells are unknown, not zero, which can overestimate slow-moving demand; managers must confirm the report's blank-cell semantics before changing this policy.

No monthly inventory cell is explicitly zero in these supplied files. Therefore **zero historical stockouts are inferred**. Future explicit zero historical snapshots support flagged comparable-period lost-demand estimates, never fabricated outage-day counts. Negative inventory adjustments do not establish stockouts. These limitations are surfaced rather than replaced with synthetic inventory, lead times, categories, prices or customers.
